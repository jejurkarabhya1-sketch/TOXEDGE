Our Services

Comprehensive toxicology and risk assessment solutions across regulated industries,
delivered with scientific rigor and regulatory expertise.

Toxicological Risk Assessment

We provide comprehensive toxicological evaluations to identify, characterize, and manage potential health risks associated with chemical and biological substances. Our expertise includes human health hazard identification and characterization, weight-of-evidence evaluations, read-across and data gap analysis, threshold and dose–response assessment, and risk characterization for intended and unintended exposure.

Our Expertise Includes:
  • Human health hazard identification and characterization
  • Weight-of-evidence evaluations
  • Read-across and data gap analysis
  • Threshold and dose–response assessment
  • Risk characterization for intended and unintended exposure
Services Include:
  • Toxicological Risk Assessment (TRA)
  • Product Safety Assessment
  • Margin of Safety (MoS) evaluation
  • Exposure-based safety assessments
  • Risk-benefit evaluation
Applicable To:
  • Pharmaceuticals & excipients
  • Impurities & degradation products
  • Food ingredients & dietary supplements
  • Cosmetics & personal care products
  • Medical devices & materials
  • Home care and consumer products

Regulatory & Safety Documentation

We provide end-to-end documentation support for regulatory submissions and compliance requirements. Our team prepares CTD Module 2 (Nonclinical Overviews & Summaries), toxicological expert reports, CPSR (Cosmetic Product Safety Report), PIF (Product Information File), safety narratives and justification documents, and regulatory response support.

Key Deliverables:
  • CTD Module 2 (Nonclinical Overviews & Summaries)
  • Toxicological expert reports
  • CPSR (Cosmetic Product Safety Report)
  • PIF (Product Information File)
  • Safety narratives and justification documents
  • Regulatory response support
Regulatory Frameworks:
  • ICH, OECD, EFSA, IARC, FDA, WHO
  • EU Cosmetics Regulation
  • REACH & CLP
  • US FDA and global regulatory pathways

Exposure & Risk Characterization

We conduct scientifically robust exposure assessments to support safety evaluations across consumer and occupational settings. Our services include consumer exposure modelling, occupational exposure assessment, aggregate and cumulative exposure analysis, PDE and OEL derivation, and scenario-based risk evaluation.

Services Include:
  • Consumer exposure modelling
  • Occupational exposure assessment
  • Aggregate and cumulative exposure analysis
  • PDE and OEL derivation
  • Scenario-based risk evaluation

Impurities, PDE & OEL Assessment

We provide expert support for impurity qualification and occupational safety evaluations in accordance with regulatory expectations. Our scope includes Permitted Daily Exposure (PDE) calculations, Occupational Exposure Limits (OELs), ICH M7 and impurity risk assessment, nitrosamine risk evaluation, and justification of limits for regulatory submission.

Scope Includes:
  • Permitted Daily Exposure (PDE) calculations
  • Occupational Exposure Limits (OELs)
  • ICH M7 and impurity risk assessment
  • Nitrosamine risk evaluation
  • Justification of limits for regulatory submission

Safety Data Sheets (SDS) & Classification

We support the preparation and review of safety documentation in compliance with global chemical regulations. Services include SDS authoring and review, GHS / CLP classification, hazard communication support, labelling and safety statements, and regulatory alignment checks.

Services Include:
  • SDS authoring and review
  • GHS / CLP classification
  • Hazard communication support
  • Labelling and safety statements
  • Regulatory alignment checks

Environmental Risk Assessment

TOXEDGE provides environmental risk assessments to evaluate the potential impact of substances on ecosystems and non-target organisms. Our assessments integrate ecotoxicological data, exposure scenarios, and regulatory guidance to support environmental safety evaluations across product categories. We support environmental hazard identification, risk characterization, and regulatory submissions in alignment with international frameworks such as OECD, REACH, and region-specific environmental regulations.

Services Include:
  • Environmental hazard identification
  • Ecotoxicological risk characterization
  • Regulatory submissions support
  • OECD and REACH compliance
  • Region-specific environmental regulations

ICH M7-Compliant Assessment

TOXEDGE provides comprehensive ICH M7–compliant assessments for pharmaceutical impurities, including mutagenicity evaluation, impurity classification, and control strategy development. Our services include in silico (Q)SAR assessment, weight-of-evidence evaluation, acceptable intake (AI) derivation, justification of control limits, and regulatory submission support.

Services Include:
  • In silico (Q)SAR assessment
  • Weight-of-evidence evaluation
  • Acceptable intake (AI) derivation
  • Justification of control limits
  • Regulatory submission support

Endpoint-Specific Toxicological Assessment

We provide endpoint-driven toxicological assessments tailored to regulatory requirements and product-specific safety concerns. This includes evaluation of developmental and reproductive toxicity (DART), genotoxicity and mutagenicity, repeated-dose and systemic toxicity, and local tolerance and irritation. Our assessments integrate available in vivo, in vitro, and in silico data to support scientifically justified conclusions.

Endpoints Evaluated:
  • Developmental and reproductive toxicity (DART)
  • Genotoxicity and mutagenicity
  • Repeated-dose and systemic toxicity
  • Local tolerance and irritation

Endocrine Disruption Assessment

TOXEDGE conducts endocrine disruption assessments in line with international scientific and regulatory frameworks. We evaluate mechanistic data, in vitro and in vivo findings, and available regulatory guidance to determine endocrine activity and adversity. Our assessments support endocrine disruptor screening and evaluation, weight-of-evidence analysis, regulatory justification and documentation, and risk-based interpretation of findings.

Services Include:
  • Endocrine disruptor screening and evaluation
  • Weight-of-evidence analysis
  • Regulatory justification and documentation
  • Risk-based interpretation of findings

In Silico Toxicology & Predictive Assessment

We apply in silico approaches to support hazard identification, data gap filling, and regulatory risk assessment. Our services include QSAR analysis, read-across justification, structural alerts assessment, predictive toxicology modeling, and OECD-compliant approaches. In silico tools are applied in alignment with OECD principles and regulatory expectations.

Services Include:
  • QSAR analysis
  • Read-across justification
  • Structural alerts assessment
  • Predictive toxicology modelling
  • OECD-compliant approaches
