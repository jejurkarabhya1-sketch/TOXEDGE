About TOXEDGE

TOXEDGE is a specialized toxicology and risk science organization providing scientifically robust, regulatory-aligned safety assessment services. Our work is driven by scientific rigor, technical expertise, and a strong understanding of global regulatory expectations.

We support organizations in navigating complex safety challenges through structured evaluation, defensible risk assessment, and high-quality scientific documentation.

With expertise spanning human health toxicology, exposure science, and regulatory toxicology, TOXEDGE delivers solutions that are practical, credible, and aligned with international standards.

Our Core Framework: A3

Assess. Analyse. Advise.

01

Assess

We systematically evaluate available data, product context, exposure scenarios, and regulatory requirements to establish a clear scientific foundation.

02

Analyse

We apply toxicological principles, weight-of-evidence approaches, and regulatory science to interpret data, address uncertainties, and derive meaningful conclusions.

03

Advise

We deliver clear, defensible, and practical recommendations that support regulatory compliance, risk management, and sound decision-making.

Our A3 framework ensures that every assessment is scientifically robust, transparent, and aligned with global regulatory expectations.

TOXEDGE is a science-led toxicology and risk assessment organization delivering high-quality, regulatory-aligned safety evaluations across regulated industries.
